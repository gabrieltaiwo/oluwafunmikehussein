As Nigerian financial, telecommunications, and technology businesses expand through holding companies and operating subsidiaries, governance friction often shifts from the parent boardroom down to subsidiary boards.

On paper, wholly owned subsidiaries appear straightforward. In practice, they sit at the intersection of group strategy, sector-specific regulation, and individual director liability under CAMA 2020.

## Common Breakdowns in Group Structures

Across corporate groups, three recurring gaps expose subsidiary directors and parent boards alike:

- **Rubber-stamped resolutions:** Subsidiary boards asked to ratify group-wide financing, guarantees, or inter-company agreements without receiving adequate commercial rationale or independent legal review.
- **Misaligned statutory calendars:** Annual general meetings, audited account approvals, and CAC annual returns falling behind schedule because local secretarial filings are treated as secondary to parent-company reporting.
- **Unclear delegation matrices:** Ambiguity over which capital expenditures, regulatory filings, or employment commitments require subsidiary board approval versus parent-level sign-off.

## Practical Controls That Hold Up

Effective subsidiary governance does not require duplicating parent-company bureaucracy across every entity. It requires clarity:

- **Standardized delegation of authority:** A concise schedule distinguishing parent reserved matters from subsidiary operational authority.
- **Centralized compliance tracking:** One statutory calendar covering CAMA 2020 filings, beneficial ownership registers, and sector regulator returns across every legal entity in the group.
- **Dedicated board induction:** Ensuring nominee and resident directors on subsidiary boards understand that their statutory duties under Nigerian law attach to the specific company on whose board they sit.

When parent oversight and subsidiary statutory discipline work together, group transactions move faster—with fewer surprises during due diligence or regulatory review.
