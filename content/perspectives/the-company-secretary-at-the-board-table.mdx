Across listed and regulated companies in Nigeria, the statutory weight carried by the Company Secretary is frequently underestimated until an AGM notice is challenged, a statutory filing lapses, or a board faces a deadlocked vote.

Under CAMA 2020 and the Nigerian Code of Corporate Governance (NCCG) 2018, the Company Secretary is not merely a scheduler of meetings. The office carries direct statutory accountability for ensuring that board procedures are respected, directors receive timely guidance on their duties, and regulatory obligations before the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC), the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), and NGX Regulation are met.

## Advisory Independence vs. Operational Pressure

Having served both as Company Secretary and as an Executive Director on the board of a publicly listed company, one tension stands out clearly: the Company Secretary reports operationally within management yet owes a fiduciary and procedural duty to the board as a whole.

Maintaining that balance in practice requires three institutional habits:

1. **Direct access to the Chair:** Agenda setting, board evaluation follow-up, and director induction cannot be filtered solely through executive management.
2. **Early involvement in transactions:** Capital raises, related-party contracts, and board transitions carry strict statutory notice and disclosure clocks. Bringing the secretariat in after commercial terms are settled invites preventable regulatory delays.
3. **Clear board charters:** Written terms of reference for the board and its committees prevent ambiguity when urgent operational decisions test the boundary between executive authority and reserved board matters.

## Governance as Institutional Continuity

Executives and non-executive directors rotate over time; statutory records and institutional memory remain. When boards treat the Company Secretary as senior governance counsel, meetings become sharper, disclosures stay dependable, and compliance ceases to be a last-minute exercise before filing deadlines.
